# Coreweaver capability roadmap: skills, connectors, and evidence-led short video

## Purpose

Coreweaver should add capabilities only when they make a **reviewable decision route** stronger. The target is not a larger automation stack or a higher posting volume. It is a coherent operating system in which an audience question becomes a source-backed page, a limited set of useful derivatives, an approved external release, and an observable learning loop.

The operating center remains Coreweaver’s content and evidence system: the Industrial Automation Applications pillar, the ARM primitives page, manual Notion review, the asset ledger, and the new Coreweaver Ops workspace. Any connector or skill should preserve the following boundary:

> **Workers may prepare research, scripts, assets, metadata, drafts, and review packets. Named humans approve publication, distribution, relationship representation, pricing, and customer commitments.**

## Capability baseline

| Capability | Current state | Role in the operating system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `content-media-production` skill | Available and validated. | Governs people-first page briefs, claim maps, asset ledgers, media atomization, and release readiness. |
| `evidence-led-short-video` skill | New reusable skill, validated locally. | Extends a reviewed page into one claim-safe YouTube Shorts or TikTok derivative. |
| Notion connector | Enabled. | Remains the controlled source of blog drafts and manual publication handoff. |
| GitHub connector | Enabled. | Supports source-controlled page, workflow, and documentation review through pull requests. |
| Coreweaver Ops workspace | Published internal control surface. | Holds work-item state, sources, claims, media records, manual approvals, and commercial-learning notes. |
| Weekly source refresh | Enabled but awaiting a recorded successful production invocation. | May mark already-recorded sources as due for manual review; it cannot publish or communicate externally. |

## Skills roadmap

The new **evidence-led short video** skill is intentionally narrow. It is not a general video factory. It requires a reviewed parent page, source/claim boundary, five-beat maximum storyboard, asset record, caption/transcript, metadata package, and manual release owner. Generated or conceptual visuals can orient or explain; they cannot be positioned as proof of real equipment, deployments, outcomes, people, or partner relationships.

| Priority | Skill | Reusable purpose | Do not include |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Evidence-led short video | Turn one reviewed page or framework into one platform-specific short-form package. | Automatic post creation, unsupported hooks, fictional footage, or implied industrial operation. |
| 2 | Coreweaver operating brief | Standardize an initial page/work-item intake across reader decision, claim boundary, source freshness, ARM link, and review owner. | Keyword-only prompts or volume targets. |
| 3 | Source-refresh triage | Turn `review_due` source records into a bounded recheck pack and a human decision. | Silent source replacement or automatic content alteration. |
| 4 | Approved relationship response | Prepare factual inbound-context briefs and non-binding response drafts. | Automated outreach, pricing, proposals, or asserted partnerships. |

The first skill is complete. The second and third should be created only after two or more Coreweaver Ops cycles show repeated drafting or triage friction. The fourth should wait until real inbound signal exists; the workspace must never be seeded with hypothetical contacts or reviews.

## Connector selection principle

Select a connector by the smallest missing operational job. Do not enable several overlapping publishers or AI media tools before the first short validates the workflow. Coreweaver already has Notion for draft control and GitHub for reviewed implementation; the next connector should either produce the editable video master or measure an approved release.

| Priority | Connector | Specific job | Default control | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Descript | Record/edit a short, retain transcript, create captions, and export an editable master. | Keep the script and asset ledger in Coreweaver Ops; export only after human content review. | Best first production connector. |
| 2 | Metricool | Review approved social performance, maintain a calendar, and prepare/schedule approved assets. | Treat it as a reporting and approval surface; do not enable unattended cross-posting. | Best first measurement/distribution candidate after one manual release. |
| Alternative | Buffer | Maintain drafts, approvals, and scheduling for YouTube Shorts; customize platform-specific copies. | Use draft/approval state; a human makes the final scheduling decision. | Choose instead of—not alongside—Metricool when a lean publishing queue is more valuable than analytics breadth. |
| Later | Canva | Produce deliberate social covers and visual cards after the asset brief is approved. | Record asset source, rights, and modification state. | Optional supporting design surface. |
| Later | Ayrshare or Blotato | API-led multi-network distribution. | Require explicit per-release approval and an audit trail. | Defer until a stable, approved distribution protocol exists. |
| Defer | HeyGen | Synthetic presenter or avatar production. | Require identity, disclosure, visual-provenance, and claim-boundary decisions. | Do not use for the first pilot. |
| Defer | Gmail or HubSpot | Inbound context and human follow-up preparation. | No automated outbound communication or commercial commitments. | Revisit after real inquiry volume justifies it. |

The current connector inventory includes Descript, Metricool, Buffer, Canva, Ayrshare, Blotato, HeyGen, Gmail, and HubSpot as dormant options; only Notion and GitHub are currently active. No connector should be enabled until its selected job is confirmed and the user completes its authorization flow.

## Short-form pilot: Industrial Automation Applications

Use the Industrial Automation Applications pillar as the first parent work item. Its Coreweaver Ops record already has four external context sources, three classified claims, a planned conceptual-media row, and pending manual approval gates. The first short should remain a **framework explanation**, not a case study or product demonstration.

### Audience, job, and boundary

| Brief field | Pilot decision |
| --- | --- |
| Intended viewer | An operations, automation, or growth leader who needs a more inspectable way to discuss system recommendations and bounded actions. |
| Viewer question | “When an industrial system observes a condition, what makes the next decision accountable rather than opaque?” |
| Core teaching turn | Observation, recommendation, permitted action, and retained record are distinct states that should not be collapsed into one claim of autonomy. |
| Parent route | The Industrial Automation Applications work item, with the contextual link contract to ARM primitives. |
| Prohibited implication | Coreweaver operates a facility, controls equipment, improves safety/reliability/performance, is certified, or is partnered/integrated with an external industrial entity. |
| Next action | Read the Coreweaver ARM primitives context or request a human-reviewed working session about one recurring decision route. |

### Forty-second storyboard

| Time | Beat | Spoken or captioned message | Visual role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 seconds | Hook | “A sensor reading, a recommendation, and an action are not the same decision.” | Simple text-led conceptual opening. |
| 4–11 seconds | Context | “Industrial automation often gets discussed as one black box. A more useful view separates what is observed from what is interpreted.” | Deterministic state diagram. |
| 11–25 seconds | Framework | “Coreweaver’s frame asks: what signal matters, what mandate permits the next step, who reviews it, and what record remains?” | Conceptual decision route; label it as a framework. |
| 25–33 seconds | Limit | “That frame does not prove an outcome or claim that a system is being operated.” | Plain-language boundary card. |
| 33–40 seconds | Route | “See the method behind the route in Autonomous Resource Management.” | Human-approved destination and a descriptive CTA. |

The narration should be original speaker footage or a disclosed, conceptual presentation treatment. Do not use industrial B-roll as if it depicts a Coreweaver system. If using generated visuals, label them internally as illustrative and retain the asset record with creator/source, rights state, modifications, and provenance availability.

## Production and release flow

1. The method editor drafts the short brief from the already reviewed Industrial Automation Applications work item.
2. The evidence steward checks the spoken script, caption file, title, description, CTA, and on-screen language against the claim records.
3. The media producer creates one editable vertical master with transcript and a documented visual treatment. Descript is the recommended first optional production connector because it supports transcript-oriented editing and captions.[1]
4. The release operator prepares a YouTube Shorts package and a TikTok-adapted package. The asset becomes a platform draft only after the media and release gates have passed.
5. A named human publishes or schedules. YouTube Shorts can be up to three minutes; the pilot remains shorter for one clear teaching turn.[2] TikTok’s Upload API creates a draft for the creator to edit and post in TikTok’s own flow, making it a safer first handoff than direct posting.[3]
6. After human release, the learning analyst records the final platform URLs, parent route, release date, platform-specific observations, and source-recheck state in Coreweaver Ops.

## Measurement and monetization connection

The pilot’s success criterion is not a view target. It should test whether a short helps the intended viewer recognize one Coreweaver decision route and choose a bounded next action. Review completion, qualified questions tied to the parent page, and requests for a scoped diagnostic or working session are more useful than raw impression count. A scheduling or analytics connector should inform this learning loop, not become a volume engine.

## Recommended next controlled build

Complete the existing **evidence and media approval gates** for the Industrial Automation Applications work item, then enable **Descript** for one pilot master. Keep publication manual. After one human-released video, choose exactly one of Metricool or Buffer for approved scheduling and performance review; do not enable both.
