## Existing outward reference layer

The cloud already uses NIST for industrial-control and manufacturing-automation context, the National Laboratory of the Rockies and U.S. Department of Energy for energy-system context, and C2PA for media-provenance context. These organizations should remain cited sources and topic-context entities, not implied partners, product endorsements, or deployed integrations.

NIST’s Manufacturing Extension Partnership identifies material handling among manufacturing-automation applications, alongside machine tending and visual inspection. This supports a material-handling content branch, but it does not support a Coreweaver claim of system performance or deployment.[1] NIST’s AI Risk Management Framework is a voluntary guide for incorporating trustworthiness considerations into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI systems. It supports a future governance/reference branch, but not a claim of NIST certification, endorsement, or formal integration.[2]

## Current operational state

PR #8, #9, #10, and #13 remain open as staged documentation, metadata, automation, or content-drafting work. These should be resolved intentionally rather than bypassed through a new content wave. The current roadmap should make published Coreweaver pages the stable center, let manually reviewed blog content become the next ring, and hold external implementation relationships behind explicit rights and governance boundaries.
