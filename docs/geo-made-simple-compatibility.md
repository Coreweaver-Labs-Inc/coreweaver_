# GEO Made Simple Compatibility Decision

## Decision

Coreweaver’s `geo-made-simple` repository is treated as a first-party design and operating reference, not as an authority for unverified public claims. The flagship adopts its strongest durable patterns: a plain-language entity explanation beside expressive brand language; crawlable page relationships; source, scope, and review boundaries; descriptive internal links; a private-next-step conversion path; and a strict distinction between an observation, a draft, and a public claim.

The flagship does **not** import service availability, mid-market positioning, named commercial offers, pricing, external profiles, case-study structures, ratings, agent behavior, trend data, research findings, or performance assertions from that repository. Those require their own controlled source and explicit approval.

## Compatible public patterns

| Pattern | Flagship adaptation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Clear entity statement | Pair the expressive “signal systems” position with a readable explanation of entity, evidence, distribution, and measurement work. | The statement describes a working method, not a promised business outcome. |
| Crawlable relationships | Retain a small public architecture: home, method note, verification boundary, and field notes, all linked with descriptive anchor text. | No empty topic hubs, placeholder resources, or fabricated proof routes. |
| Source-aware publication | Show source/date/scope language on notes and maintain an honest zero-post state when the editorial source is unavailable. | No private Notion content, raw visitor data, or model-generated assertions are published. |
| Evidence boundary | Continue to distinguish observations, human review, and assertions and show what a public page does not establish. | Coreweaver makes no certification, compliance, security, technical-deployment, or performance claim through this surface. |
| Identity system | Apply deep obsidian, smoked graphite, oxidized brass, restrained teal, and steel editorial contrast from the supplied Coreweaver identity direction. | The approved organization mark remains the entity anchor; imagery is not evidence of product status or outcomes. |

## Explicit exclusions

The following `geo-made-simple` patterns remain out of scope for the flagship until separately sourced and approved: a three-service offer catalog, mid-market buyer claims, service-pricing metadata, case-study proof records, comparative positioning, client statements, ranking/citation language, trend-triggered content, automated drafting or publishing, and any claim of AI agent, ledger, consensus, compliance, or verification capability.

## First implementation slice

The flagships’s first compatible slice is a **public source-and-method map** in the information architecture, rather than a large content hub. It makes the site’s method pages, evidence boundary, public notes, explicit claim boundary, and a private working-session path discoverable in ordinary HTML. This is the smallest durable improvement because it clarifies entity context and page relationships without manufacturing a volume of unsupported content.
