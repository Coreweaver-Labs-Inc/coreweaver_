# GEO-COMPASS research notes

## Principles adopted

Google Search Central emphasizes helpful, reliable, people-first content, original analysis, clear authorship, accurate sourcing, and a visible explanation of who, how, and why content was created. It warns against mass-produced, search-engine-first content and unsupported freshness tactics. Structured data should describe visible page content, use complete and accurate properties, and be validated with the Rich Results Test.

The user-provided AI Mastery reference organizes the intelligence stack into model systems, knowledge systems, agentic compute, production infrastructure, and trust infrastructure. Its loop is Acquire → Retrieve → Reason → Act → Observe → Verify. These ideas map well to Coreweaver’s content silos: concepts, evidence, workflows, implementation, measurement, and verification.

The attached GEO-COMPASS standard adds three qualifiers for durable pages: a proprietary concept, an original analytical framework, or a canonical definition. It also requires cross-linking, external citations, standalone FAQ answers, authorship, and anti-slop review.

## Guardrail

Coreweaver should not promise rankings, citations, or follower growth as guaranteed outcomes. It should publish evidence, make claims inspectable, and measure discovery and citation outcomes over time.
