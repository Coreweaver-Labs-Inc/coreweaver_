# Truth Ledger — Societal-Value Positioning Framework

## Status

**Working positioning draft — not a public product claim or legal remedy.** This document translates the founder-supplied societal-value narrative into language that can guide Coreweaver and ARM communication only after page-specific evidence, naming/rights review, and release approval.

## The durable thesis

When machine-mediated answers influence how people discover, evaluate, and trust an organization, the underlying record must be easier to inspect, correct, and contextualize. **Truth Ledger** is the working name for a proposed correction-oriented evidence layer: a versioned record of claims, sources, relationship context, and corrections that people and machines can inspect.

The thesis is not that a ledger can force a model to change, erase an accurate public record, guarantee a commercial outcome, or establish legal truth. It is that a source-aware, attributable, and revisable public record gives people a better basis for evaluating information than unsupported repetition.

> **Core proposition:** Make a claim’s source, context, correction history, and responsible owner easier to find before an answer, buyer journey, or agent action treats it as settled.

## Evidence-led message architecture

| Layer | Message | What it means in practice | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem | Machine-mediated discovery can compress a reader’s path from question to conclusion. | Information may be evaluated through answers, listings, summaries, and citations before a person visits a source. | Do not claim a particular model made or repeated a particular error without a documented test and release review. |
| Principle | **Correction should be inspectable.** | Show the current claim, source, date, responsible owner, correction state, and link context. | An inspectable record is not a guarantee of truth, model update, removal, or legal effect. |
| Method | **Evidence before persuasion.** | Separate facts, interpretations, frameworks, and promises; cite factual content; maintain a correction and freshness path. | Sources inform a claim; they do not create an endorsement, certification, or partnership. |
| Governance | **Mandate before action.** | Identify who may propose, approve, release, correct, or escalate a record change. | This is a design method, not a claim that delegated agent authority is already technically enforced. |
| Outcome | **More legible trust decisions.** | People, buyers, and future automated systems can inspect a better record before acting. | Never promise a deal result, model ranking, safety outcome, reputational recovery, or transaction approval. |

## Public narrative draft

### Short form

**Truth Ledger is a proposed evidence layer for machine-mediated trust.** It makes claims, sources, corrections, and responsibility more inspectable so people and systems can distinguish a supported record from an unsupported assertion.

### Website opening

> **When an answer stands in for research, the record behind it matters.**
>
> Truth Ledger is a working approach to making public claims easier to inspect, contextualize, and correct. It connects source-backed evidence, structured relationships, correction history, and accountable review. The aim is not to suppress accurate information or promise control over any AI system. The aim is to give people and future automated systems a clearer record before they act on a claim.

### Method statement

The method has four public components: **Signal**, the current claim and its context; **Attestation**, the source or accountable assertion behind it; **Checkpoint**, a versioned change or correction state; and **Ledger**, an inspectable record that links these elements. These are a communication and governance framework. They do not by themselves establish that a claim is true, safe, legal, complete, or accepted by a third party.

### Ethical distinction

The relevant distinction is not positive coverage versus negative coverage. It is **inspectable evidence versus unsupported assertion**. A correction-oriented record should preserve relevant context, link to primary sources where available, label changes, and leave an accountable review path. It should not fabricate signals, hide valid information, create fake reviews, or describe a source relationship that does not exist.

## Release gates for public use

The phrase **Truth Ledger** must remain a draft label until its naming/rights status and product scope are reviewed. Every real correction story must be permissioned, sourced, and reviewed for privacy, legal, and reputational consequences. Any buyer-statistic claim must link to the original research and its methodology. Any model-behavior assertion must name the version, date, prompt conditions, source mode, and observed result; do not convert a single test into a general claim.

Do not publish the following phrases without controlled evidence and subject-matter review: “engines can actually read,” “every claim has a receipt,” “court docket,” “attested schema,” “stops losing deals,” “AI stopped saying,” “without a human in the loop,” “rogue agents,” “traceable, budgeted, reversible,” “nothing overwritten blindly,” “everything reversible,” or “the record of what’s true stays.” These phrases either overstate technical capability, make a legal or commercial outcome claim, or imply a deployed system that is not currently evidenced.

## Controlled next action

Create a **Truth Ledger / Evidence Record** source-backed concept page only after the naming decision and claim map are approved. Its initial call to action should invite a scoped evidence-map working session rather than promise remediation, suppression, correction by an AI model, or agent-mediated transaction authority.
