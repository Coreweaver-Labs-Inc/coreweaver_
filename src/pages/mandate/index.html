Core concept
Mandate Chain
A mandate chain is a cryptographically-signed chain of authority that links every AI agent action to the human directive that authorized it. In Coreweaver Labs' GBrain framework, every agent operates within a scoped mandate — a bounded permission delegated by a sovereign human operator. Actions outside the mandate scope trigger Graceful Escalation; they never proceed silently. Every mandate is logged to the Truth Ledger with a Merkle-verifiable timestamp, creating an immutable audit trail from execution back to the original human principal.

The mandate chain answers three questions in under one second: Whose authority authorized this action? What was the exact scope of that authority? When was it granted?
Truth Ledger
The Truth Ledger is GBrain's immutable audit layer — an append-only log where every agent action is written with cryptographic attestation. It is Merkle-verified, queryable in under one second, and EU AI Act compliant under Article 12 logging obligations. The Truth Ledger is not a monitoring tool — it is a production kernel primitive. Every node in the AURE swarm writes to it continuously.
